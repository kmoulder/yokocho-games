# Earth data

The first [Earth playground pass](earth-progression-plan.md) adds 227 authored fleet, district, and landscape clusters on this atlas. Their anchors and exaggerated sizes are gameplay approximations, not additional surveyed map data. The underlying shorelines and geographic datasets remain unchanged.

`src/data/earth.json` is generated by `node scripts/build-earth.mjs`. The
builder fetches the sources below at build time, simplifies their geometry,
and writes the compact runtime contract used by the roll-up world. The current
snapshot is 0.59 MiB with 41 regions, 176 country land entries, 24 lakes, and
72 cities.

```text
{
  origin: [longitude, latitude],
  regions: [{ id, name, ring: [[x, z], ...], kind, group }],
  land: [{ id, name, continent, rings: [[[longitude, latitude], ...], ...] }],
  lakes: [{ id, name, ring: [[longitude, latitude], ...] }],
  cities: [{ name, lon, lat, rank }]
}
```

The metro rings use the NYC Borough Boundary FeatureServer, including the
five boroughs and substantial satellite islands. New Jersey, Pennsylvania,
Delaware, Connecticut, Rhode Island, Massachusetts, mainland New York, and
Long Island use the
10m Natural Earth state/province geometry so the surrounding regional atlas
tile remains filled and the nearby coastline remains recognizable. The global
land rings, representative inland lakes, and populated places use the official
Natural Earth Vector GeoJSON repository at 110m scale. Natural Earth
data is public domain; the NYC service is a public municipal data service.

Local rings are projected to metres around `[-73.9665, 40.7740]` with the
same equirectangular approximation as Manhattan: longitude is scaled by
`111320 * cos(origin latitude)` and latitude by `111320`, with south as +Z.
The geographic rings use their WGS84 longitude/latitude coordinates and are
rendered on that same equirectangular flat atlas so the roll-up can continue
without a projection break. This is intentionally stylized and increasingly
distorted at global scale. Growth now opens space without a separate globe
pickup; these rings are not a globe projection or navigation data.

Natural Earth's country polygons retain their source ring winding and holes
as separate rings. They are deliberately coarse and are intended to read as
continent silhouettes rather than coast-navigation data. City points are the
Natural Earth `WORLDCITY` set plus a short curated supplement for Boston,
Philadelphia, Montreal, Vancouver, Houston, San Diego, Lima, Kolkata, and Ho
Chi Minh City; `rank` is Natural Earth's scale rank plus one, where lower is
more prominent.

The borough building layer is a separate compact snapshot in `src/data/boroughs.json`, regenerated with `npm run data:boroughs`. It uses actual NYC building locations, oriented footprint bounds, and roof heights, with a representative low-rise sample. See [borough building source and sampling notes](../src/data/boroughs-sources.md). The runtime instances nearby tiles, collects buildings independently of the Manhattan land gate, and removes them when their parent terrain is gathered. OID-addressed bit pages preserve picked buildings in existing version-1 journey saves.
